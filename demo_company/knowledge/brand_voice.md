# Brand Voice and Welcome Document Guide

## Tone
- Warm, direct and plain-spoken. Address the new hire by first name. No corporate jargon such as synergy, leverage or circle back.

## Welcome document contents
- A greeting that names the person and the department.
- The start date, written out in full.
- What the first week looks like: Monday intro with People Ops, laptop and accounts setup, team lunch on Wednesday.
- Who to contact with questions: people-ops@acme.test.
