# Start-Date Reminders SOP

Owner: People Ops. Applies to requests to remind new hires about their upcoming start.

## Procedure
1. Sign in to AcmeHR with the ops service account (see the Systems Guide).
2. Read the employee directory and select active employees whose start date is within the requested window, counting from today. Include only people whose start date is today or later. People who already started are never reminded.
3. For each person, send one email to their work email. Address them by first name, state their start date written out in full, and mention the first-week plan from the Brand Voice guide. Keep it short and warm.
4. Every email needs human approval (see the policy); send emails one at a time so each can be reviewed.
5. Never send a person more than one reminder per request.

## Completion criteria
- Each person in the window has exactly one reminder email in the outbox, addressed to their work email.
- Nobody outside the window received an email.
